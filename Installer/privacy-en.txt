Privacy

Zeph-Iris runs on your PC. It has no account, no telemetry and no ads, and it does not collect personal data.

On its own, the app contacts only:
- GitHub (api.github.com): checks for a new version, at most once a day. Turn off: Settings > "Check for new versions automatically".
- Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org) and Openverse (api.openverse.org): search photos of your parts by their model names. Turn off: Devices > "Find device photos online automatically".

Phone notifications (ntfy), phone/web remote, AI advice (Ollama) and OpenRGB lighting do nothing until you set them up.
"Report a problem" only makes a zip on your PC and opens the GitHub issue page; nothing is uploaded automatically.

Setup installs PawnIO (a signed driver for reading sensors and setting fan speeds). The setup wizard in the app registers an auto-start task when you leave "Start with Windows" on. The uninstaller removes them.

Full privacy policy: https://github.com/acdclove1234-commits/Zeph-Iris/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
