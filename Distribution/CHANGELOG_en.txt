Zeph-Iris change log (newest first)
===================================

■ What's new in v6.15.10 (song changes, graphics cards, instrument recognition)
  - Gapless playlists: far fewer flashes in the previous song's rhythm during the first ~2 seconds of a new song.
    With Spotify the app checks for a song change every 0.25 s (other players every second) and flashes on kicks
    until it finds the new song's beat (while "Remember songs already heard" is on).
  - Fixed hi-hat sparkles in "Sparkle" being hard to see on graphics cards the app writes to directly (and other
    slow-updating devices such as memory).
  - "Instruments" on graphics cards the app writes to directly now moves more, so the changes are visible.
  - Graphics cards the app writes to directly now show "Graphics card: direct writes (up to N per second)" (auto is
    10 per second; it used to say 5), and you can choose up to 60 updates per second.
  - "Instruments" and "Parts play their roles" mistake sustained notes for singing much less often, so guitar and
    keys show up more.
  - "Recognize instruments with AI" now uses CED-Tiny instead of YAMNet. It tells guitar and keys apart better and
    the installer is smaller. It checks the last 2 seconds of sound every second. Instrument colors now change a
    few seconds later, so they flip back and forth less.
  - Fixed the beat AI and the instrument AI slowly drifting in time when the audio output runs at 96 kHz, 192 kHz
    or other rates. The beat now lines up the same at any rate.
  - Because of this, each song in the song memory is relearned once the next time it plays (that first time it
    does not line up from the start).

■ What's new in v6.15.9 (music sync on the beat)
  - "Color change on beat" and "Pulse" now light on the beat. While the tempo is found they flash once per beat,
    brighter on beats with a kick and brightest on the first beat of a bar (flashes on the beat went from about 40%
    to about 90%). While the tempo isn't found they flash on kicks, and parts without drums move gently with the bass.
  - Fixed kicks being missed more often since 6.15.8 (back to about the 6.15.7 level).
  - Fixed gapless playlists: the previous song's tempo no longer makes the next song hard to lock onto (the app
    forgets the old tempo when the song changes).
  - Fewer brief flickers when the color changes. On fast LED devices the color change now lands on the beat.
  - "Instruments" on graphics cards and memory no longer stays almost one color: it swells on the beat in the color
    of the instrument that stands out.
  - With "Reduce flashing" on, strong flashes are also capped at 3 per second right before they are sent.
  - Each lighting style now says which sounds it reacts to.
  - Removed the online search for device photos. On its own the app now only contacts GitHub (update check).
    Images you picked yourself still work.

■ What's new in v6.15.8 (lighting and temperature precision)
  - Less flicker in music sync: in every style, LEDs no longer wobble back and forth in brightness.
  - Hi-hats and snares now light after about 30 ms instead of about 50-60 ms.
  - Bass notes are much less often mistaken for kicks.
  - "Parts play their roles" has a new "Instrument split" choice. "By instrument" lights kick, snare, hi-hat, bass,
    vocals, chords and pads separately ("By pitch", the old look, stays the default).
  - New style "Instruments": the kick lights everything, the snare one side, hi-hats small sparks, the bass the far
    end, the voice the overall brightness, guitar and keys a flowing light. Even with a single color you can tell the
    instruments apart by brightness, position and motion.
  - "Recognize instruments with AI" (off by default): AI (YAMNet) recognizes the instrument that stands out in each
    part of a song and changes the colors slowly (blue for vocals, gold for brass, ...). It runs only on your PC.
  - Temperature control: Target mode hunts much less, and a one-off temperature spike no longer changes fan speed.
  - Curve hysteresis no longer keeps fans fast after the temperature has dropped (after 20 s it eases down).
  - Fan calibration waits for the speed to settle before measuring (a little longer for slow fans) and detects the
    low range where the speed barely changes.
  - Auto setup no longer stops on a brief load dip. If a run ends before it could measure enough, no curve is applied
    and the current curve stays.
  - Diagnostics warn when a curve reads the wrong kind of sensor (for example a "GPU" curve reading the SSD) and offer
    a one-click fix.
  - New "Guide" button that opens the manual, and a hint with a link when OpenRGB is not installed.

■ What's new in v6.15.7 (first-time setup)
  - The setup wizard ends with "Start with Windows (recommended)", on for a first setup. Before, autostart stayed off,
    so after a restart the fans silently went back to BIOS control.
  - If no fan headers are found, the wizard explains why and what to try, and can finish with temperature monitoring
    only (fans you already set up are kept).
  - AIO pumps are harder to mistake: a fast-spinning "fan" gets a hint, and it is not slowed down too much until it is
    measured.
  - Headers at 0 rpm start as "Not used" but switch back to "Fan" when they spin (for example during "Test"); the
    summary lists them too.

■ What's new in v6.15.6
  - Music sync, "Remember songs": a song now counts as the same song in any player app, so a song remembered in
    Spotify works right away in YouTube Music and others. Songs remembered before are moved over the next time they
    play (no need to learn them again).
  - If Windows' media info cannot be read for a while, the app tries again a minute later (no restart needed).

■ What's new in v6.15.5
  - Music sync: "Remember songs" now also works with YouTube Music, Apple Music, browser players and others, not only
    Spotify. Songs are told apart by Windows' media info (the name shown when you press a volume key); titles are
    never saved. Songs Spotify already remembered keep working.

■ What's new in v6.15.4
  - Music sync: the beat AI now gives back its working memory after each analysis. While music played, the app's
    memory grew to about 1 GB; now it barely grows (the analysis is only about 10% slower).

■ What's new in v6.15.3 (failures shown on screen, FanControl import)
  - Failures that were only written to the log now appear as notices in the app (and as a tray notification while the
    window is closed): settings that cannot be saved (retried every 30 s, with a notice once saved), a power plan that
    cannot be switched, a watchdog that cannot start or keeps stopping, OpenRGB unreachable for 2 minutes, hotkeys in
    use, a firewall rule that cannot be added, a fan that cannot be handed back to BIOS, settings from the previous
    version that cannot be carried over, and failed settings backups/imports, game records, incident records and reports.
  - When the settings file cannot be read, the notice now says whether another app had it open, it could not be written
    back, or it was damaged.
  - FanControl import: matches the current FanControl file format (hysteresis, step up/down speeds and the minimum speed
    are carried over). Choosing a file that FanControl has not saved yet now says so.

■ What's new in v6.15.2 (update safeguards, safety review)
  - Updates: if the preparation for going back to the previous version fails, the update is not started (the current
    version keeps controlling the fans).
  - Updates: a new version counts as working after 90 s of continuous fan control. A new version that hangs and does
    not exit is closed by force, fans are set to the safe speed, and the previous version is put back. A sleep in
    between no longer rolls back a working version.
  - Going back to the previous version keeps the current settings in the backups folder first ("Before restore" in
    Settings [設定] -> "Safety & backup" [安全・バックアップ]).
  - Watchdog: after repeated crashes it waits a little before restarting (for example during a graphics driver swap),
    and it sets the motherboard fans to the safe speed first.
  - When a temperature sensor disappears while hot, the fan no longer slows below what it last needed.
  - Game records: temperatures cover the same period as FPS, stalls over 1 s are counted and shown, and the 1% low
    method is explained.
  - Phone remote: the access key is removed from the address bar, and failed-key counting is stronger.

■ What's new in v6.15.1
  - When a graphics card is found but none of its temperatures can be read (this can happen after the graphics driver
    was replaced), a notice is shown at start. Reinstalling the latest driver from the maker's site and restarting the
    PC often fixes it (built-in CPU graphics are not included).

■ What's new in v6.15.0 (game records, automatic rollback after a failed update, safety review)
  - Game records (Report [レポート] page, off at first): each time you play, one record is kept with the average FPS,
    1% low (stutter), CPU and GPU temperatures and fan speed. FPS is measured with the bundled Intel PresentMon
    (MIT License, Intel-signed). It never sees screen contents or input. Records stay on this PC.
  - If an updated version does not start (for updates from 6.15.0 on): before updating, the current version and settings are kept in the previous
    folder, and if the new version has not started controlling the fans after 8 minutes, the previous version is put
    back automatically. Double-click previous\ZephIrisRecovery.exe to go back by hand.
  - The update installer's digital signature is checked (a signed version is never replaced by an unsigned one or one
    from another publisher).
  - If OpenRGB is not running, Zeph-Iris starts it with the SDK server on (OpenRGB installed in Program Files, or the
    OpenRGB service).
  - History [履歴] graph: new "Smooth" [なめらかに] toggle (display only; records are unchanged).
  - Sensors [センサー] page: min, max and average, with "Reset min/max".
  - "Hardware report…" [動作報告…]: opens a GitHub hardware report filled with the part names and the number of sensors
    and fans read (you send it yourself).
  - Calibrating a fixed-speed fan without a temperature curve stops as soon as any watched temperature can no longer
    be read, and does not start when no temperature can be read at all.
  - With two GPUs of the same model, the second one's temperatures are watched for emergency full speed too.
  - Music sync: after audio capture restarts, beat-AI results from the earlier audio are not used.
  - PNY graphics card LEDs are written directly only on verified boards (by PCI ID).
  - Settings rows no longer cut off long translations.

■ What's new in v6.14.5
  - Music sync, "Parts play their roles" [パーツごとの役割]: with several fan headers, every header used to get the bass
    (and the same color). Zones of one device that get the same sound are now given bass, mids and treble in turn
    (zones with a chosen "Sound to react to" [反応する音] are kept).
  - ARGB headers on ASUS, MSI and Gigabyte motherboards are also treated as fans and start from the bass.

■ What's new in v6.14.4
  - Music sync: "Pulse" [パルス] now looks clearly different from "Beat color change" [ビートで色替え]: its color no longer
    switches on the beat but shifts slowly, and its afterglow lasts longer.
  - Device photos are now also looked up on Openverse (a search for freely usable photos), not only Wikimedia Commons.
    Devices with no photo found are searched again every 30 days.
  - Photos of a different product with the same model number (such as "980" vs "980 PRO", "5070" vs "5070 Ti") are no
    longer used.

■ What's new in v6.14.3
  - The logo at the top left of the window no longer has the dark square behind it; only the artwork's light shows.
  - "Remembered songs" on the Lighting page [ライティング] now goes up as soon as a song is remembered, even while the page
    stays open (it used to change only when the page was opened again).

■ What's new in v6.14.2
  - The logo at the top left of the window is larger and drawn from the icon's largest image, so it no longer looks blurry.

■ What's new in v6.14.1
  - The app icon now uses the author's artwork as is, at every size.
  - After updating, shortcuts and the taskbar could keep showing the old icon; setup now rebuilds Windows' icon cache.

■ What's new in v6.14.0 (beat AI for music sync, remembered songs)
  - Beat AI (Beat This!, JKU Linz): every few seconds the AI checks the latest audio and fixes the lights if they follow the
    wrong tempo or the off-beat. Runs only on this PC. On 20 real songs: in sync 83% -> 91%, first lock 13.3 -> 8.2 s
    (flashes on the beat unchanged at 80%).
  - Remember songs already heard: the beats the AI found are remembered per song and matched from the start the next
    time (second play 92%, 7.0 s, almost no AI work). Songs are told apart by a code made from the title in Spotify and
    similar apps; titles and audio are not saved. Both can be turned off on the Lighting page [ライティング].
  - Beat tracking keeps the beat when the kick drops out for a moment and returns from the off-beat more easily
    (29 real songs: 75% -> 82%).
  - AI cooling advisor: fixed the screen freezing while it answers. The app now computes the suggested numbers with fixed
    rules and the AI only explains them, so it no longer suggests faster fans when there is plenty of margin or changes
    to BIOS-controlled fans.
  - New app icon.
  - The installer is about 13 MB larger because of the beat AI.

■ What's new in v6.13.0 (easier to switch from other apps)
  - Import from FanControl [FanControl から取り込む…]: reads the curves and fan names from FanControl (Rem0o) settings (the .json in its
    Configurations folder) into a new profile. The button is on the Fan curves page and in Settings -> Safety &
    backup. Headers and sensors are mapped to this PC first. Imported fans start with control off; check the curves,
    then turn them on (Undo takes it back).
  - Conflict warnings: when software that drives fans or LEDs is running (Armoury Crate, MSI Center, iCUE, L-Connect,
    NZXT CAM, SignalRGB, FanControl and others), the app says what it fights over (fans or LEDs) and how to make
    them work together. LED-only software is reported only while lighting is on. It checks every 2 minutes on any page.
  - A "Hardware report" form on GitHub: reports from parts not tried yet help a lot, even when everything works.

■ What's new in v6.12.0 (lighter, season-aware, easier to report)
  - Lighter: direct LED writes to PNY graphics cards kept almost one CPU core busy. They are now limited to 10 per
    second (up to 30 in the settings), rest between writes, and skip LEDs whose colour barely changed. The
    memory line in the log also shows CPU use. The exe is no longer compressed, which saves about 60–100 MB of
    memory (the download is a little larger).
  - Season reminder: when the room is 5°C or more warmer than when auto setup measured (without a room sensor:
    the CPU idle temperature is 6°C or more higher), the app suggests measuring again (at most every 30 days).
  - "Report a problem" [不具合を報告…] is now at the bottom of the sidebar and in the tray icon's menu.
  - Music sync: Spectrum no longer rises in steps on a kick either (less flicker).
  - Fan curves: the list shows "Fan · CPU Fan" instead of the header's internal ID.
  - Installer: shows what the app sends over the network (update check, device photos) and how to turn it off
    before installing (privacy policy: PRIVACY.md).

■ What's new in v6.11.1 (music sync tuned on 24 recorded songs)
  - Tempo tracking: in parts where hi-hats or chords are strongest between the beats, the predicted beat could stick
    exactly between the real beats. If the kicks (bass) are clearly stronger half a beat away, it now shifts half a
    beat (time on the off-beat: 15% → 9% on average). Songs with a dotted or shuffle feel no longer take a minute
    or more to lock (26.6 → 13.7 s on average).
  - Pulse, Beat hue, Mix, Role: a kick flash no longer rises in steps (bright → a little darker → brighter), which
    looked like flicker (about half the flicker).
  - Beat hue: bass between the beats is toned down so the beat stands out. Chase: the bar brightens on each beat.
    Sparkle, Ripple, Chase, Scroll: calmer in quiet parts, bigger in the chorus.
  - Device photos: frames from review videos and charts are no longer used as product photos; ones picked earlier
    are removed and searched again.

■ What's new in v6.11.0
  - Auto setup (the "Auto setup" page, formerly "Test & tune"): one button measures fans not measured yet, then
    measures with the CPU, then with the GPU, and builds the quietest curves that still hold the target
    temperatures (about 20–30 min; you check the result before applying it).
  - The app now loads the GPU itself, so there is no need to start a game or benchmark.
  - Measured fans get their minimum speed raised just above the speed where they stop (never lowered).
  - The CPU and GPU targets are set separately. "Apply to all profiles" uses +5°C for Silent and −5°C for
    Max cooling.
  - Renamed: "Auto-tune" is now "Auto setup", "Target temperature (automatic)" is "Target temperature",
    "Auto calibration" is "Fan characteristics" ("Measure fan"), and "Create automatically…" on the curve page is
    "Auto setup…".
  - A run that measured nothing no longer reports "unreachable even at full speed (°C at full speed)".
  - The GPU load uses Vortice (MIT License); its licence is in the licenses folder.

■ What's new in v6.10.4
  - Fixed the "Sync test" (Music sync → "Audio delay" row) never lighting the LEDs. The click fades in
    smoothly, and its quiet start was taken for noise, so no click was ever detected.
  - During the test only this app's own click is captured, so it works with music playing. On Windows versions
    that cannot capture a single app, the whole output is captured as before; pause the music there.

■ What's new in v6.10.3
  - Music sync: "Sparkle" now brightens on the beat predicted from the tempo (it used to wait for a detected kick,
    so it lagged a little and missed beats where no kick was found), with a stronger beat pulse.
  - Hi-hat sparkles, toned down too far in 6.9.1, are partly back (moderate: 70% brightness, at least 0.12 s apart).
  - Installer: the official installer of PawnIO, the fan control driver, is now included. On PCs without PawnIO it
    is installed without the internet (winget remains the fallback). PawnIO's licence (GPL) and the location of its
    source code are in the licenses folder.
  - Monitor: the "Memory (RAM)" tile showed memory including virtual memory (the page file), e.g. "43.6 / 53 GB"
    on a 32 GB PC. It now shows physical memory only.

■ What's new in v6.10.2
  - Lighting: removed the faint flicker left in Lightning, Waves and the other new effects. Dim LEDs no longer
    alternate between neighbouring values; each keeps the value it last sent until the brightness clearly changes.
  - Report a problem: Settings [設定] -> General -> "Report a problem…" makes a zip on the desktop with the logs,
    settings and hardware list (user name, PC name, folder names, email and IP addresses, and the phone
    notification address and passcode are hidden) and opens the GitHub report form with the version and hardware
    filled in. The app sends nothing by itself.

■ What's new in v6.10.1
  - Lighting: removed the flicker from Lightning, Waves, Birdsong, Fire, Aurora, Meteor and Heartbeat. Dark areas
    are now smoothed like the other effects (except near black) instead of jumping in steps, and Heartbeat and
    Aurora no longer blink off and on at low brightness. Fire flickers a little more slowly.

■ What's new in v6.10.0
  - Lighting: seven new effects: Lightning, Waves, Birdsong, Fire, Aurora, Meteor and Heartbeat. All follow the
    speed setting; Waves and Heartbeat use the chosen colour. They can also be chosen per device and per zone (for
    example, only the keyboard as Meteor). Lightning and meteors strike on every device at the same moment.
    Graphics cards show a still picture, as with the rainbow (turn on "Animate rainbow and breathing too" to let
    them move).
  - Safety: re-asserting full speed wrote a value just over 100% (100.001%); it now stays within 100% (fan speed
    was not affected).
  - Quality: tests now run automatically on GitHub (build, over 2,000 tests, the translation check and a check
    that opens every page), plus a six-hour fan control run and a long run of every effect.

■ What's new in v6.9.1
  - Music sync: "Sparkle" no longer over-reacts to hi-hats. The brightness, number and spacing of hi-hat sparkles
    follow the intensity (moderate: 55% brightness, at least 0.16 s apart; calmer still with "Reduce flashing").
    The treble parts of "Parts play their roles" no longer flash on every closely spaced hi-hat either.
  - Lighting: a "Fans" setting per zone splits fans daisy-chained on one header (channel) so each fan can have
    its own colour and light mode (Lian Li UNI HUB channels, fans on a splitter cable).
  - The lighting page explains why fans on a Lian Li UNI HUB in MB Sync cannot have separate colours, and how to
    let OpenRGB drive the hub directly.

■ What's new in v6.9.0
  - Lighting: each device and each zone (motherboard header) can have its own colour and light mode (solid,
    rainbow, breathing, temperature, off). Example: graphics card solid red, motherboard white, only the radiator
    fans in rainbow, everything else in music sync. Lighting [ライティング] -> Devices: "This device's colour"; zones
    under "Open color matching and per-zone settings".
  - Fixed: the sync test's LEDs could stay dark when the audio source was set to one app (such as Spotify),
    because the app's own click was not captured. During the test the whole output is captured.
  - Devices page: devices without a photo are looked up by model on Wikimedia Commons (freely licensed photos),
    and only photos that clearly show the same product are used automatically, with the author and licence shown.
    Only the device names are sent; it can be turned off at the top of the page.
  - The image button can now find a photo online or paste a copied image: right-click an image in your browser ->
    "Copy image", then paste.
  - Illustrations of devices without a photo show the maker's colour and name.

■ What's new in v6.8.2
  - Electricity cost: the power supply's losses are now included (Settings: "Power supply efficiency", 88% by
    default). Before, they were left out, so the estimate read 10-20% below a wall-outlet measurement; with the
    same settings it now reads a little over 10% higher than before.
  - Settings [設定] -> Notifications & integrations -> Electricity cost: calibrate with a plug-in power meter. Enter
    the reading at idle and press "Record" to work out the power of everything besides the CPU and GPU; record
    under load too (during a game, for example) to work out the power supply efficiency. "Clear calibration"
    goes back to the starting estimate.
  - The report page says "an estimate within about ±20%" before calibration and shows the calibration date after.

■ What's new in v6.8.1
  - Safety: with a fan curve on an SSD temperature, playing music or copying files could make the app report
    "the temperature sensor is stuck" and run every fan at 100%. The "load changed but the temperature did not"
    check now only uses CPU and GPU load, where load moves the temperature within minutes (an SSD's load is
    read/write activity, and its temperature moves in 1 °C steps).
  - Lighting: the live spectrum shows 7 gridlines instead of 3 (display only; audio analysis is unchanged).

■ What's new in v6.8.0
  - New name: Fanlume is now called Zeph-Iris (another service already uses the name Fanlume).
  - It installs to C:\Program Files\Zeph-Iris. When Zeph-Iris starts for the first time, it takes the settings and
    records of Fanlume (or ProFanController), moves the start-at-logon entry and the firewall rule for the phone
    view to the new name, and then uninstalls Fanlume while keeping its settings and records (so the old name
    leaves Installed apps).
  - The "Update" button in Fanlume 6.7.1-6.7.3 does not find the new name, so run the 6.8.0 installer yourself
    once. From 6.8.0 on, "Update" works again.
  - Exported settings files are now *.zeph-iris.json (older *.fanlume.json and *.profan.json files still load).
  - Thanks: the About section in Settings and the licenses folder now thank the open-source projects Zeph-Iris
    is built on.

■ What's new in v6.7.3
  - Installer: if PawnIO (the fan control driver) is missing, the installer installs it from the official winget
    source (needs the internet; if that fails, the app's setup can still install it as before).
  - Updating from inside the app: the installer left in the temp folder by each update (about 74 MB) is deleted
    on the next start. For 14 days after an update, Settings [設定] -> General [一般] -> Updates [アップデート] ->
    "Go back to the previous version" fetches the previous installer from GitHub, checks it the same way and
    goes back. If a just-updated version closes unexpectedly, the notice bar offers this too.
  - Diagnostics (cooling): the "Room temperature sensor" can be chosen (automatic, none, or any temperature
    sensor). If the motherboard's "System" sensor measures the board rather than the room, choose none or a
    sensor closer to the room for a better check on days without idle time.
  - The "Open the releases page" button now looks like the other buttons.

■ What's new in v6.7.2
  - Installer: with "Launch Zeph-Iris" ticked on the last page, the installer could show "CreateProcess error:
    code 740" and Zeph-Iris did not start (when the installer was started by double-clicking). It starts now.

■ What's new in v6.7.1
  - Updating from inside the app: Settings [設定] -> General [一般] -> Updates [アップデート] now has "Check for
    updates" and "Update". "Update" downloads the new installer, checks that it matches the file published on
    GitHub (size, SHA-256 and version), and replaces the app without showing any screens, then starts it again
    (the fans run on the BIOS settings for about 10-20 seconds).
  - With "Check for new versions automatically" on, the app asks GitHub at most once a day and tells you when a
    new version is out (only the app's name and version are sent; you can turn it off).
  - This works from 6.7.1 on. To go from 6.7.0 to 6.7.1, run the installer yourself.
  - Diagnostics (CPU / GPU cooling): days without idle time (CPU power high all day) were compared with values
    worked out a different way, so it could say cooling got worse when it had not. Only like is compared now.
  - The Settings tabs stay at the top while you scroll. The sidebar and the mini widget show the name Zeph-Iris,
    and the history CSV and exported settings files are named Zeph-Iris too.

■ What's new in v6.7.0
  - New name: ProFanController is now called Fanlume (now Zeph-Iris). Settings and records are carried over.
  - Installer: Fanlume now comes as an installer (Fanlume-6.7.0-setup.exe). It installs to
    C:\Program Files\Fanlume, adds a Start menu entry, and can be removed from Installed apps in Windows.
    To update, run the newer installer: it closes the running Zeph-Iris properly first and can start it again
    afterwards; settings, logs and records stay. 2_更新.bat (update) is gone.
  - Moving from ProFanController: the installer closes a running ProFanController properly (the fans go back
    to the BIOS settings). When Zeph-Iris starts for the first time, it takes the settings and records from
    C:\Tools\ProFanController and moves the start-at-logon entry and the firewall rule for the phone view to
    the new name. You can delete C:\Tools\ProFanController afterwards.
  - Exported settings files are now *.fanlume.json (older *.profan.json files still load).

■ What's new in v6.6.1
  - First run: started from Downloads, the Desktop or inside the zip, the app now copies itself to
    C:\Tools\ProFanController and starts again from there (before, it only showed a "move it" notice
    and might not work properly). Settings used at the old place are carried over. Placed anywhere
    else, it runs in place as before. The setup wizard installs PawnIO by itself when it is missing
    (once; if that fails, the buttons and the official-site link are shown as before).
  - Languages: the chosen language now reaches every part of the screen (the sidebar, page headers
    and many buttons stayed Japanese before), and it switches right away, without restarting the app.
    Fan control, LEDs and music sync keep running.
  - Music sync: very quiet sounds (for example faint highs in a bass-heavy song) no longer flash as
    hi-hats or snares. A higher Beat sensitivity [反応の感度] brings the detail back.
  - Music sync: the spectrum and the "Spectrum" style use the 10 bands of a common graphic equalizer
    (31 Hz-16 kHz), with the frequency under each bar. The other styles flash at the same moments as
    before.
  - Safety: if the app ends unexpectedly and the watchdog fails to set the safe fan speed (for example
    when the exe was replaced while running), it still restarts the app. Before, the watchdog could stop
    too and leave the fans at their last speed.

■ What's new in v6.6.0
  - Languages: the user interface is now available in 18 languages (English, Japanese, Chinese
    Simplified and Traditional, Korean, Russian, Ukrainian, German, French, Spanish, Italian,
    Portuguese (Brazil), Polish, Dutch, Turkish, Vietnamese, Thai, Indonesian). The phone view and
    the AI advisor's answers follow the same language. The translations are new, so some wording
    may read unnaturally. This English guide is included, and the updater and the PawnIO installer
    show English messages.
  - Lighting [ライティング]: Undo and Redo on the Lighting page (also Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y). "Reset to
    defaults" can be undone too; the integration switch, lights off and the connection are not.
  - Fans: the emergency full speed now always watches the CPU temperature and the GPU core and
    hot spot, even when no curve uses them (for example when the radiator fans follow the coolant only).
  - Music sync: can listen to one app only (for example Spotify), so other sounds and notification
    sounds do not trigger the lights. Better beat tracking (on 30 recorded songs the lights followed the
    beat 44% -> 66% of the time, and flashed on the wrong beat 6% -> 4% of the time), twice as many snares
    found, melodies and high notes glow in the "beat colour" style, new per-part and solid-colour styles,
    "lights off" that keeps the control running, and "reset to defaults" for the reaction values.
  - Music sync, timing:
    - The song's tempo is remembered, so the beat comes back quickly after a busy passage; a beat
      grid that stays shifted by the same amount is corrected.
    - Slow songs (about 80-90 BPM) flash at double speed less often (on 31 recorded songs, time
      at double speed 9.9% -> 4.5%, flashes between beats 13.0% -> 10.4%). In exchange, fast songs
      (about 150-160 BPM) sometimes flash on every other beat; those flashes stay on the beat.
    - Snares reach the lights 11 ms sooner.
    - With the Windows sound format at 96 kHz, 192 kHz and so on, beats and snares are found as
      well as at 44.1 / 48 kHz (snares were missed and some songs locked at double tempo).
    - The output device kind (onboard, USB, HDMI / DisplayPort, Bluetooth, virtual) is shown on
      the Lighting page. For Bluetooth, HDMI and virtual devices it suggests an Audio delay
      [音の遅れの補正] that you can apply with one button. When audio software such as Nahimic, Dolby,
      DTS, SteelSeries Sonar or Voicemeeter is running, it names it and points to the Sync test
      [同期テスト].
  - Diagnostics [診断]: the fan dust and wear check moved here and was made more accurate (room
    temperature and fan-curve changes no longer look like dust; sudden and gradual changes are told apart).
    The AI cooling advisor is now an option, off by default (switch at the top of Diagnostics).
  - PawnIO can be installed from inside the app; a clean uninstall was added; the updater checks the
    version and origin of the zip and rolls back on failure; the watchdog process and the bundled font
    are lighter.
